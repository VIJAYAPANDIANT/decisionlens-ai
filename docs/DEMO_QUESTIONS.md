# Demo Questions

Use these exact questions during the live demo or video recording. The sample dataset (`sample-data/sales_data.csv`) is confirmed to support the answers to these questions because it contains revenue, date, category, and regional columns.

**1. Why did revenue change?**
*Tests the AI's ability to interpret the deterministic revenue growth metric and relate it to the previous period.*

**2. Which category performed best?**
*Tests the AI's ability to read the category performance breakdown and cite the highest revenue category.*

**3. Which region needs attention?**
*Tests the AI's ability to identify the lowest performing region from the regional analysis and suggest strategic focus.*

**4. What should the business focus on next?**
*Tests the AI's ability to synthesize the overall dataset health, the insights generated, and provide a holistic business recommendation without hallucinating numbers.*

*(Note: Do not hardcode expected answers, as the AI will dynamically generate them based on the precise numerical context passed by the backend. The numerical evidence will always be 100% accurate.)*
