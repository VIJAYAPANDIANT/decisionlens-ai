# 3-Minute Demo Script

**0:00–0:20 | PROBLEM**
> "Businesses have huge amounts of data, but often struggle to turn raw CSV files and business data into understandable, actionable decisions. AI is powerful, but asking an AI to analyze numbers directly often leads to hallucinations."

**0:20–0:40 | UPLOAD**
*(Upload the sample dataset `sales_data.csv`)*
> "DecisionLens AI first analyzes the data using deterministic Pandas and NumPy calculations. We separate the math from the AI."

**0:40–1:05 | DASHBOARD**
*(Show KPIs, Revenue trend, Category/region performance)*
> "These numbers come directly from the uploaded dataset. We have our total revenue, orders, average order value, and revenue growth instantly visualized."

**1:05–1:30 | TRACEABLE INSIGHT**
*(Open an insight. Click 'View Evidence')*
> "Every important insight is traceable back to the underlying business data. Here we can see exactly what was observed, the supporting metrics, the source analysis, and its limitations."

**1:30–2:10 | AI ASSISTANT**
*(Ask: "Why did revenue change?")*
> "Gemini is not calculating the business metrics. The verified Pandas analysis is passed to Gemini as context, so the AI explains the verified results rather than inventing numbers. You can see the AI correctly cites our verified evidence."

**2:10–2:35 | RECOMMENDATION**
*(Show AI recommendation section)*
> "Based strictly on the data, the AI generates actionable recommendations, distinguishing between verified fact and AI interpretation."

**2:35–2:55 | WHAT-IF**
*(Open What-If Simulator. Select: +10%)*
> "Next, we can explore assumptions. If sales change by 10%, we can see our projected revenue and estimated change. This is a transparent scenario calculation, not a machine-learning forecast. You can see the exact algebraic formula traced at the bottom."

**2:55–3:00 | ENDING**
> "DecisionLens AI turns business data into verified insights, traceable evidence, AI explanations, and actionable scenarios. Thank you!"
